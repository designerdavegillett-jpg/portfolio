If it looks wrong after pasting

      
Everything is one size and one color. You pasted as plain text. Undo and paste again normally.

      
The font looks different. Expected. Gmail and Outlook do not load web fonts, so most people see Arial. It is designed for that.

      
A broken image icon. The logo address is wrong or not public. Switch the logo off and paste again.
